Immigration counsel that has sat inside your HR team

Most immigration problems don't start in the filing. They start in the handoffs between HR, the employee, and outside counsel — a missed deadline, a form field nobody double-checked, an I-140 that quietly closes off every future visa category for a key engineer. We built this practice after 12 years working exclusively in employment-based immigration for tech companies, including a stint as in-house immigration counsel at a 3,000-person company. So we design the workflow around how HR teams actually operate, not around how law firms prefer to bill. When you sponsor foreign national talent, your case belongs to a specialist, not a paralegal you'll never meet.

What we handle

1

H-1B & L-1 Sponsorship

End-to-end management of your specialty occupation and intracompany transfer petitions, from lottery strategy through approval. We run the cap-season timeline so HR isn't chasing signatures in March.

2

PERM & Green Card Processing

This is the labor certification stage where a single mislabeled requirement can trigger a denial and cost you your hire. We manage recruitment steps, prevailing wage, and I-140 filings as one connected process.

3

O-1 Extraordinary Ability Petitions

For the researchers, founders, and senior engineers who don't fit the H-1B mold. We build the evidentiary record that USCIS actually credits, so strong candidates aren't lost to a weak petition.

4

Immigration Compliance Audits

Public access files, I-9s, and LCA posting obligations reviewed before a government inspection finds the gaps. We tell you where you're exposed and how to close it.
